# Pokémon Sleep Scoring

Use signed, reproducible calculations. A neutral nature contributes `0`; beneficial effects add points and harmful effects subtract points. Do not convert a nature into a standalone 0–100 tier before combining it with the rest of the individual evaluation.

## Required references

- Read [references/scoring-rules.md](references/scoring-rules.md) before calculating, explaining, or changing a score.
- Use [scripts/nature-scores.js](scripts/nature-scores.js) for the complete 25-nature ingredient-specialist table and the species-specific berry-specialist tables.
- Use [scripts/species-scores.js](scripts/species-scores.js) for the complete ingredient- and berry-specialist species scores, the skill-specialist framework, every current main-skill family, their eight-hour operation models, and skill-slot economics. Recovery uses a standard `1 healer + 4 primary producers` team: the healer's own recovery weight is `0`, the other four targets' weights are `1`, and `Lv.6 Energy for Everyone S effective 18.1×4 = 72.4` anchors single-use practical value `100`. Direct Energy uses `Lv.7 Charge Strength M 6,858 Energy = 82`. For every non-healer skill specialist, calculate net slot output as `gross skill index + own ordinary-output index - replaced fourth producer index`; a formal healer pays no slot cost. Treat weaker or mixed recovery skills as conditional healers and retain both healer-slot and extra-skill-slot scenarios until the team can rely on them alone. The default fourth-producer benchmark is provisional and dynamically selects the fourth of the top four final-form berry producers at Lv.70 with 50% favorite-Berry share; actual team calculations must override it. Do not add the legacy ordinary-production 10% again after using slot-adjusted net output. The neutral Dream-Shard bridge uses internal skill SP only as a stable calibration clue: `Lv.8 fixed Dream Shard 7,303 ÷ Lv.7 Charge Strength M 6,252`, giving `2,500 shards = 95.8` and `1 shard = 0.0383139` resource-value points. Keep pure Dream Shards out of current-week Snorlax Energy; Aura Sphere adds its direct-Energy component separately. Permanent species scoring uses demand coefficient `1.0`; team/event scenarios may pass `--dream-shard-demand`. Ingredient-acquisition uses the current ingredient-weighted Lv.1 fixed-recipe multiplier and keeps equal-pool probabilities provisional; Super Luck combines ingredient and Dream-Shard components, Hyper Cutter keeps its `53/328` large-success sample provisional, and Present keeps teammate Candy separate until probability and resource value are supplied. Tasty Chance, Cooking Power-Up, and Cooking Assist use 21-meal models; keep `mature-standard` as the generic comparison profile and `user-activity` as a separate personal event scenario. This user's `user-activity` Cooking Power-Up profile defaults to Good Camp and must use actual surplus-ingredient availability; Tasty Chance is unaffected by Good Camp. Cooking Power-Up must enumerate each 1.78-recipe capacity threshold and separate filler-only Energy from recipe-tier upgrade Energy. Metronome uses the current provisional equal 25-outcome pool and runs nonlinear cooking outcomes through their own diluted weekly state models. Skill Copy must use four actual teammate effects at the copier's skill level, not the targets' native levels. All-Mighty outputs all 12 selectable skills separately; keep its selected-skill trigger rate and Candy completion pending unless supplied. Skill/all specialists can store two triggers; berry/ingredient specialists use a 78-help pity ceiling and store only one. Random recovery defaults to four productive targets out of five and accepts `--team-size` / `--productive-targets` overrides. Team-dependent skills must be recomputed from the actual team when known. If a future main skill is not in the relevant table, keep the affected final score pending instead of filling it with zero.

## Working rules

- Distinguish confirmed rules from provisional or undecided rules. Never silently turn a provisional value into a confirmed one.
- Preserve multiplicative interactions. Do not reduce speed-plus-probability combinations to simple addition.
- Score legal subskill-seed potential only. An S skill can upgrade when its upper form is absent; if S and M coexist, both remain and score separately.
- Use final-form base data for berry-specialist ingredient-probability effects.
- Apply ingredient-pattern scoring only to ingredient specialists and all-rounders. For berry and skill specialists, mark this component as not applicable and contribute `0`, not a free baseline score.
- For ingredient specialists, keep the species score unchanged and apply the ingredient-combination coefficient only to the non-species individual-quality block. Do not multiply the species score or a signed nature component by this coefficient in isolation.
- Evaluate ingredient combinations primarily for long-term Lv.70 use with confirmed coefficients: `AAA 1.00`, `ABB 0.85`, `ABA 0.80`, `AAB 0.70`, `AAC 0.70`, `ABC 0.50`.
- For this user's Lv.70 box score, use `final score = final-form species score × 50% + individual score × 50%`. Calculate the individual block as `clamp(weighted subskill raw × 70% + signed nature score / 55.6 × 30, 0, 100)`; for ingredient specialists only, multiply that nonnegative block by the confirmed ingredient-pattern coefficient. A neutral nature contributes `0`, not a hidden baseline. Apply legal seed upgrades first, retain S+M as two distinct slots, and assign multiplicative interaction beyond simple addition to the later relevant slot. Map unevolved helpers to their chosen final form; evaluate base Eevee across all eight evolutions and disclose the highest-score route. Keep Mew and Darkrai pending until the all-rounder formula is confirmed. Use `scripts/box-scores.js` to generate the website snapshot and disclose every provisional subskill bridge in its tooltip.
- For ingredient specialists, evaluate species inventory with the confirmed eight-hour unattended model, final-form carry after evolving from the first stage, theoretical AAA occupancy, and a standardized `2.0×` average Energy speed. Berry specialists have no separate inventory module because post-capacity Sneaky Snacking continues berry Energy. For skill specialists, do not award raw inventory points; include capacity, two-trigger storage, and eight-hour collection loss only in main-skill operation friendliness.
- For ingredient specialists, the confirmed species structure is production `80%`, final-form inventory `10%`, and main-skill synergy `10%`; production is ingredient-count efficiency `60%` plus ingredient-base-strength efficiency `40%`.
- For berry specialists, the confirmed species structure is Lv.70 berry production `90%` plus main-skill synergy `10%`.

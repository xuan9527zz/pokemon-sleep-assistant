# Ingredient-helper selection

## Current executable rule

Compare same-species target ingredient production with blank `1.00` using help speed × ingredient probability. Main-skill output and Skill Level Up do not enter this multiplier.

Apply the Helping Bonus/no-Helping-Bonus thresholds in [scoring-rules.md](scoring-rules.md).

Route handling:

- AAA keeps the multiplier grade.
- ABB is capped at B; it is not automatically promoted to B when its multiplier is C.
- Other mixed routes are C for long-term individual evaluation.

Ingredient Finder M/S, Helping Speed M/S, Helping Bonus and relevant Nature modifiers multiply rather than add. Legal Sub Skill Seed upgrades and a Plain Mint neutralization are reflected in potential multiplier.

## Operational use

A C-grade long-term route can still produce ingredients today, but it does not become a long-term individual grade because of existing investment or an unusual route. Team and weekly production calculators may still use its actual current output.

## Evidence boundary

Earlier course material described AAA rarity, Lv.30 workers and species-specific ABB exceptions. The user’s current universal ABB cap and non-ABB mixed-route C rule supersede the previous route coefficients and course gates for the displayed individual grade.

# Berry-helper selection

## Current executable rule

Conventional berry helpers and Berry Burst helpers are separate models, but both occupy the berry role.

- Conventional helpers compare natural berry energy against the same-species blank.
- Berry Burst helpers compare natural berry energy plus expected skill-generated berry energy.
- Berry Finding S is required for any S/A/B result.
- Ingredient-down Nature is positive for conventional berry output; Ingredient Finder and ingredient-up Nature are negative.
- Helping Bonus adds 5% self speed in the individual multiplier and additional team value in full-team calculations.

Conventional grade thresholds and Berry Burst thresholds are defined in [scoring-rules.md](scoring-rules.md). Berry Burst may reach S without Helping Bonus; label that build as selfish rather than rejecting it.

## Speed-cap context

Helping Bonus and Helping Speed subskills share the 35% reduction cap; speed Nature applies outside it. In a lineup already carrying four Helping Bonuses plus Helping Speed M, Helping Speed S contributes only the remaining one percentage point. For Berry Burst, Skill Trigger S can therefore beat Helping Speed S in that context. Never generalize this into “Helping Speed S is always useless”; calculate actual cap headroom.

## Evidence boundary

Earlier course frames supported Berry Finding S as the berry-position entry requirement and explained the value of a Helping Bonus backbone. The user’s current multiplier thresholds supersede the earlier course-derived blanket rule that every Berry Burst S helper must have Helping Bonus.

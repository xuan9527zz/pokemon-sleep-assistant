# Skill-helper selection

## Current executable rule

All ordinary skill helpers use the recovery-helper individual table. Compare expected triggers per day against the same-species blank, including speed, skill probability, pity and timely collection.

- With Helping Bonus: S `>=2.00`; A `1.70–<2.00`; B `1.60–<1.70`; otherwise C.
- Without Helping Bonus: never S; A `>=1.84`; B `1.75–<1.84`; otherwise C.

Skill Level Up S/M and non-output resource subskills are neutral (`1.00`) in this trigger multiplier. Their separate resource value may still matter in team or investment tools.

Berry Burst helpers do not use this table; evaluate them as berry roles using [berry-selection-guide.md](berry-selection-guide.md).

## Species hunt scope

The current species tier table controls which skill species are worth actively hunting. At present the user’s ordinary skill hunt list emphasizes group recovery, Dedenne and the holiday Spheal entry, plus separately tiered mythical helpers. An unlisted skill species defaults to species C even if an individual panel has a strong multiplier.

## Evidence boundary

Earlier course material separated formal healers, persistent helpers and short-deployment tools. Those distinctions remain useful for explaining team use, but the user’s current individual multiplier thresholds supersede separate panel-graduation formulas.
